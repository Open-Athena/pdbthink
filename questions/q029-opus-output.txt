## Analysis

Scanning inter‑residue heavy‑atom contacts, most close approaches in this helical‑hairpin chain are in the normal 3.5–4.5 Å range (e.g. Leu33–Leu37 ≈ 4.0 Å, Trp44–Leu41 ≈ 3.6 Å, Asp32–Lys36 ≈ 3.6 Å).

One contact, however, is grossly too short, between the Gln56 side‑chain amide and the Asp60 carboxylate (an i, i+4 pair on the same helix):

| Atom pair | Distance (Å) |
|---|---|
| B:Q56 **NE2** – B:D60 **OD2** | **1.73** |
| B:Q56 NE2 – B:D60 CG | 2.83 |
| B:Q56 NE2 – B:D60 OD1 | 3.70 |
| B:Q56 NE2 – B:D60 CB | 3.67 |

An N···O separation of 1.73 Å is far below the sum of nitrogen and oxygen van der Waals radii (~3.1–3.3 Å) — even shorter than a covalent N–O bond — so this is by a wide margin the most severe steric overlap in the model, with several supporting sub‑3 Å contacts between the same two side chains.
