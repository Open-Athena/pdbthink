## Analysis

I scanned for abnormally short inter‑residue contacts, focusing on long/charged side chains that pack against each other.

**Key finding – Lys79 / Glu83 (helix D):**

- LYS79 NZ = (−20.005, 13.499, 11.971)
- GLU83 OE1 = (−20.674, 14.623, 13.568)

Δ = (−0.669, 1.124, 1.597) → **d = 2.06 Å**

For an N···O pair the van der Waals sum is ≈3.1 Å (even an ideal salt‑bridge/H‑bond is ≈2.7–3.0 Å), so this contact is ~1 Å too short — a severe overlap. Supporting contacts in the same pair are also tight (NZ···CD = 3.02 Å, CE···OE1 = 3.00 Å), confirming the two side chains are interpenetrating rather than merely hydrogen bonded.

Other candidate close contacts examined are all within acceptable ranges, e.g.:
- MSE1 CE···LEU6 CD1 = 3.67 Å
- LYS3 NZ···LYS46 O = 2.59 Å (normal H‑bond)
- TYR34 CE1···ALA54 CB = 3.65 Å
- LEU37 CD2···ALA54 CB = 3.66 Å
- MSE66 CE···LEU62 CD1 = 4.55 Å
- THR29 OG1···ASP27 OD1 = 2.82 Å (normal H‑bond)

Hence the single most severe steric clash is between the Lys79 ammonium group and the Glu83 carboxylate.

FINAL: A:K79--A:E83
